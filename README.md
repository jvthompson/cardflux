# Cardflux
Cardflux is a multiplayer, virtual tabletop software built in Flutter, designed to accomodate playing a variety of different card games for up to four players online.

Documentation will appear at some point. Still very much a work in progress, so a lot of things may change.

## About AI Usage
To any considering downloading this application, I just want to say that AI did the bulk of the heavy lifting on this project. This was my first time working in Flutter, and I had been wanting a project to see just how good AI coding could be. I just want to be upfront about that, in case you are someone who is absolutely against all use of AI. I am a developer by day, and built this as a quick experiment and to test the capabilities of AI development, although in my day to day work I do write my own code.
